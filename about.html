About KINGDAO

A factory in Yixing, and an engineering team that designs what the factory builds.

We design the plant and we supply the equipment inside it — the core lines engineered in Yixing, Jiangsu and produced to our drawings, the wider catalogue produced to the same drawings and specification. The process design and the equipment come from the same team.

In short: Founded 2006 and based in Yixing, Jiangsu, China. The core equipment lines are engineered in Yixing and produced to our drawings; the wider catalogue follows the same drawings and specification. Design track record: 19 projects across 10 industries, 250 to 100,000 m3/d, including a 15,000 m3/d special economic zone plant in Cambodia and a 20,000 m3/d tannery estate in Bangladesh.

What we do

WuXi KingDao Environmental Technology Co., Ltd. designs wastewater and water treatment plants and supplies the equipment that goes into them. Our engineering base in Yixing, Jiangsu covers the core lines — screening and comminution, sludge dewatering, biological treatment, tertiary polishing, dosing and packaged plants — and across the wider catalogue we supply to our own drawings and specification.

Because the design team and the factory sit in the same company, a customer deals with one technical party from the first mass balance to the start-up report. That is unusual in this industry, where equipment is normally bought from one supplier and the process design from another.

Where we have built

Our reference projects are in China, Cambodia, Bangladesh and Vietnam — municipal plants, tanneries, dyeing and textile parks, PCB and electroplating parks, food processing, livestock and water reuse. Capacities range from 250 m³/d packaged units to 50,000 m³/d municipal works. Each design is written to the discharge standard that applies where the plant is built — GB, TIS, QCVN, BIS or the client's own specification.

For overseas projects we work as an equipment manufacturer and process design partner behind EPC contractors, system integrators and industrial park developers. We supply under your own brand when that is what the market requires.

How we quote

A quotation lists only the lines that fit the project. We would rather quote four items that are right than twenty that make the page look complete. Motors and control panels are built to the destination grid — 380–415 V 50 Hz, 440–480 V 60 Hz, or any other voltage and frequency your local standard requires.

Common questions

What buyers ask about working with us.

When was the company founded and where are you based?

Founded in 2006. We are based in Yixing, Jiangsu, China - the same town that supplies much of the country's water treatment equipment, which is why the component supply chain here is deep.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

A design house with supply behind it. The core lines are engineered in Yixing and produced to our drawings; the wider catalogue is produced to the same drawings, and it ships under one technical package and one warranty.

What is the design track record?

19 projects across 10 industries from 250 to 100,000 m3/d, delivered as process design and technical consulting: feasibility study, preliminary design, construction drawings and commissioning support.

What overseas references can you point to?

A 15,000 m3/d special economic zone plant in Cambodia, a 20,000 m3/d tannery estate in Bangladesh, and an industrial park plant in Vietnam covering dyeing, resin buttons and plating at 1,900 m3/d plus 400 m3/d of plating wastewater.

Who do you normally work with?

EPC contractors, engineering companies, and industrial park owners who keep the process design and need the hardware behind it. We also supply under the customer's own brand where that suits them.
