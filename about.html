About KINGDAO

A factory in Yixing, and an engineering team that designs what the factory builds.

We design the plant and we supply the equipment inside it — the core lines built in our own plant in Yixing, the wider catalogue produced to our drawings and specification. The process design and the equipment come from the same team.

What we do

WuXi KingDao Environmental Technology Co., Ltd. designs wastewater and water treatment plants and supplies the equipment that goes into them. Our plant in Yixing, Jiangsu covers the core lines — screening and comminution, sludge dewatering, biological treatment, tertiary polishing, dosing and packaged plants — and across the wider catalogue we supply to our own drawings and specification.

Because the design team and the factory sit in the same company, a customer deals with one technical party from the first mass balance to the start-up report. That is unusual in this industry, where equipment is normally bought from one supplier and the process design from another.

Where we have built

Our reference projects are in China, Cambodia, Bangladesh and Vietnam — municipal plants, tanneries, dyeing and textile parks, PCB and electroplating parks, food processing, livestock and water reuse. Capacities range from 250 m³/d packaged units to 50,000 m³/d municipal works. Each design is written to the discharge standard that applies where the plant is built — GB, TIS, QCVN, BIS or the client's own specification.

For overseas projects we work as an equipment manufacturer and process design partner behind EPC contractors, system integrators and industrial park developers. We supply under your own brand when that is what the market requires.

How we quote

A quotation lists only the lines that fit the project. We would rather quote four items that are right than twenty that make the page look complete. Motors and control panels are built to the destination grid — 380–415 V 50 Hz, 440–480 V 60 Hz, or any other voltage and frequency your local standard requires.
