Solutions by Wastewater Type

The wastewater decides the process — not the machine we want to sell.

Twelve wastewater types we have designed plants for. Each one has a route behind it, and a reference you can check.

01Municipal wastewater · 市政污水 Challenge: Nitrogen and phosphorus limits, seasonal load variation, and a discharge permit that does not move. Typical route: Inverted AAO or modified Bardenpho → high-density clarifier → filtration → disinfection. 50,000 m³/d Xingyang · 15,000 m³/d Sihanoukville SEZ · 100,000 m³/d Xinzheng Longhu (staged)
02Tannery and leather · 制革皮革 Challenge: Chromium, sulphide, high salinity and high ammonia — the four that break a conventional plant. Typical route: Chrome and sulphide separated at source → equalisation → coagulation-sedimentation → A+B oxidation ditch. 20,000 m³/d Dhaka Tannery Estate, Bangladesh
03Textile, dyeing and printing · 印染纺织 Challenge: High colour, high salinity, PVA desizing water and poor biodegradability. Typical route: Segregated desizing and dyeing streams → coagulation/DAF → hydrolysis-acidification → AAO → reuse at 50%. 35,000 m³/d Shanquan (50% reuse) · Vietnam industrial park
04PCB and electronics · 线路板与电子 Challenge: Copper, nickel, cyanide and chelated metals across several process lines. Typical route: Stream segregation → chemical reduction and precipitation per stream → DAF + Fenton → aerobic biological polishing. 20,000 m³/d Suichuan Phase I · 30,000 m³/d Phase II
05Electroplating · 电镀 Challenge: Chelated metals that will not precipitate with simple lime dosing. Typical route: Batch segregation by metal → reduction/precipitation → ion exchange or membrane concentration where recovery is required. Vietnam industrial park · 400 m³/d mixed plating stream
06Slaughterhouse · 畜禽屠宰 Challenge: High oil, high blood content, high COD and a B/C ratio that fluctuates with the kill schedule. Typical route: Grease separation → equalisation → anoxic-aerobic → dewatering. 1,200 t/d Changshu · 700 t/d Zhangjiagang
07Livestock farming · 畜禽养殖 Challenge: High ammonia, high suspended solids, strong odour, and a site that is often far from any sewer. Typical route: Solid-liquid separation → anaerobic digestion (optional) → A/O → polishing. 280 m³/d Sansheng · 250 m³/d Xinpu
08Down and feather processing · 羽绒加工 Challenge: Feather, fine solids and surfactant load from washing lines, mixed with park domestic sewage. Typical route: Screening and grit removal → equalisation → coagulation → biological treatment → dewatering. 20,000 m³/d Lu'an Guzhen (20+ factories in one park)
09Water reuse · 再生水回用 Challenge: A reuse target that has to be met every day, not on average. Typical route: Biological treatment → membrane or filtration → disinfection; staged by quality tier so industrial and landscape demand are served separately. 30,000 m³/d Karamay Baijiantan · 50% reuse at Shanquan
10Water supply treatment · 供水处理 Challenge: Seasonal turbidity spikes and a turbidity limit below what conventional filtration reliably holds. Typical route: Inclined plate settling → V-filter → immersed ultrafiltration. 50,000 m³/d Nanzhao No.2 waterworks; turbidity <0.1 NTU
11River and waterbody rehabilitation · 河道水体治理 Challenge: Diffuse pollution, low dissolved oxygen and no space for a conventional plant. Typical route: Packaged AO fixed-bed units with grit separation, phosphorus removal and enhanced oxidation. 300 m³/d Puyuan Road river restoration
12Industrial park and SEZ · 工业园区与特区 Challenge: Mixed tenants, mixed streams, one discharge permit — and a drainage tariff that has to recover the investment. Typical route: Stream segregation by tenant type → shared biological train → reuse where justified; plant sized against the tariff model. Sihanoukville SEZ (Cambodia) · Suichuan Industrial Park · Vietnam industrial park
