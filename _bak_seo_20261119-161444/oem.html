OEM & Partnership

Your brand. Our factory. Your projects get stronger.

Our role is to add capacity behind yours — the equipment you do not build, the process design your team can lean on, and commissioning support at the far end.

Who this is for

  • Equipment suppliers and distributors who want to extend their range without carrying stock or funding tooling.
  • EPC contractors whose projects need stages outside their usual scope — dewatering, dosing, screening, aeration.
  • System integrators who need a second, factory-direct supply source when a project timeline is tight.

What we supply

Any of our 17 equipment lines, built to your specification and labelled with your brand. Typical OEM scope: multi-disc screw presses, crushing grilles, bar screens, dosing systems, aerators, mixers, MBR modules and packaged plants.

What comes with it

  • Factory pricing with stable lead times, quoted against your order pattern
  • English documentation: GA drawings, IOM manuals, material certificates, test records
  • Process calculations and equipment selection when a client's wastewater falls outside your usual range
  • Commissioning support, or remote guidance for your own service team
  • Motor supply to your grid — 380–415 V 50 Hz, 440–480 V 60 Hz, or any other voltage and frequency you specify

What we ask

An honest indication of the volumes you expect, and whether you want neutral supply or your own branding. That is enough to quote properly.
