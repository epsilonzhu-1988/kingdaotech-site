Technical description

A buried station, built in the factory, installed in one lift.

GRP cylinder, submersible pumps, pipework, valves, level control and control panel — assembled, wired and pressure tested before it leaves the plant. On site the work reduces to excavation, bedding and connection.

Where it is used. Domestic sewage collection in housing estates and rural communities; sewage collection and transfer for municipal treatment plants; stormwater collection in low-lying urban areas; construction and rehabilitation of municipal sewer networks; upgrading and extension of existing stations; water circulation in lakes and waterbodies.

Made to the duty. Every station is designed from the client's own data — flow, head, ground conditions, power supply — so the duty point is met rather than approximated by the nearest standard size.

Cylinder. Filament-wound GRP, computer-controlled continuous winding for uniform wall thickness. Every cylinder is 100% leak tested before dispatch. Ring stiffness 10,000 N/m², axial tensile strength 200 MPa.

Everything inside is pre-fitted. Pressure pipework in stainless steel 304, pressure tested before dispatch; internal service platform for valve operation; guide rails for pumps and grille so either can be lifted out by one person; ventilation and anti-slip access cover; precast steel lifting lugs on the cylinder.

Level control. Hydrostatic pressure sensor in a stainless steel 304 protection tube, with monitoring relay and remote communication — the station runs automatically on level, and reports to wherever you want the signal.

Life and access. GRP cylinder with stainless steel 304 internals, maintenance-free in normal service, with a design life beyond 20 years. Only the access cover and control panel remain at ground level; a landscape kiosk version is available where the site requires it.

Pumps and grinder

What is specified inside the station.

Submersible sewage pumps

Motor protection IP68, insulation class F or better. Bearing life over 100,000 hours, mechanical seal life over 20,000 hours, 25,000 hours fault-free under automatic control, 25-year design life.

Condition signals

The pumps report over-temperature, leakage, bearing temperature and humidity to the control system, which acts on them immediately rather than waiting for a failure.

Integrated grinder

Mounted at the inlet in place of a basket screen: solids are shredded to 6–10 mm in the flow, so there is no raking, no basket to lift and no pump blockage. Same body, materials and control as our channel grinders.

Optional basket screen

Where a project specifies a basket rather than a grinder, the station can be supplied with one — but the grinder is the reason maintenance visits drop.
