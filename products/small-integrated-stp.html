Small Integrated Sewage Treatment Plant

Packaged plants from 5 to 200 m³/d, skid or container mounted.

For a hotel, a school, a factory canteen or a small estate, a conventional concrete plant is disproportionate: too much civil work, too much operator attention, and a footprint nobody wants to give up. A packaged plant arrives as a unit, treats to the required standard and is commissioned in days.

The process inside is chosen against the discharge standard and the load pattern — AO or MBBR for nitrogen, with the tank volume set by the retention the biology actually needs, not by what fits in the box.

5 – 200 m³/dSized to the application rather than rounded up to a standard plant.
Skid or containerRelocatable, and inspectable before it leaves the factory.
Low operator attentionAutomated aeration and sludge return; the operator checks, not operates.
Standards matchedProcess selected against the discharge standard that applies on site.

How we quote this line

Sized against the duty, not picked from a stock list.

This equipment is built to the process requirement rather than offered as a fixed catalogue of standard models — because the wrong tank volume, plate size or membrane area cannot be corrected later on site.

What we need from you

Flow rate, the medium and its concentration, the installation arrangement, the discharge standard, and the supply voltage and frequency.

What you receive

A model selection with the dimensioned GA drawing, material specification, utility consumption and the price against that specific duty.

Why not a table

A published table invites selection by flow alone — which is how undersized equipment gets installed. We would rather size it with you once.

Typical lead time

15–35 days from drawing approval, depending on the line and the material. Confirm with us before quoting your client.
